# Flight Patterns & Identification
- **Reed Cormorant vs White-breasted Cormorant flight formations**:
	- Reed cormorant (*Microcarbo africanus*) often flies singly or in loose, unstructured clusters close to the water surface.
	- White-breasted cormorant (*Phalacrocorax lucidus*) frequently flies higher in organized, aerodynamic V-shaped formations over longer transit distances.

# Morphology & Plumage
- **African Darter feather wetting mechanism**:
	- When hunting, the African darter (*Anhinga rufa*) lets water penetrate non-waterproof feathers (less preen oil application), creating a "weight-belt" / wet sponge effect that reduces buoyancy and enables silent underwater stalking.
	- After hunting, perches with wings outstretched (drying posture) and re-applies water-repellent preen wax from the uropygial gland.
