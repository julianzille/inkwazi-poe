# Species Notes

### Southern African Rock Python (*Python natalensis*)
- Largest snake in Southern Africa (up to 6m, 65kg)
- Reproduction: Females produce 30-60 tennis-ball sized eggs in underground burrows/termite mounds; 3-month incubation where mother coils around eggs and stays at nest site for 2 weeks post-hatching
- Seasonality: Reduced metabolic activity during winter; courtship and mating occur in winter months

### Leopard Tortoise (*Stigmochelys pardalis*)
- Behaviour: Move to open areas before rain events (common folklore/field observation)
- Afrikaans name: *Bergskilpad*
