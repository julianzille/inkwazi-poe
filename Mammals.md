#### Leopard
##### Cubs
- cubs may be left hidden for several days while mother goes on foraging trips
	- after capturing prey and having first feed, mother returns to lead cubs to kill
- cubs moved every 2/3 days to new shelters
- while moving with cubs, tail curled upwards towards tip
- suckling cease after ~3 months
- after 9.5 months, join mother on hunt; after 11 months, make their first kill; at 13 months gain independence (siblings stay together for 2-3 months)
