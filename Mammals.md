#### Elephants
- age largely determined by teeth -- will go through 6 sets (last erupts at 30 years). Once warn out, life will end where vegetation is soft
- musth: 
	- younger bulls sporadic (weekly); from 30 years old, yearly for 2-5 months at a time
	- heightened testosterone -- looking for female mating partner
	- dribble masks from urine
	- sticky secretion from eyes stains cheeks
	- *"mischievous"* in Hindu
	- when large bull in musth stays with a herd, usually means he is in contortship with estrous female
	- other males usually give way to bull in musth
	- female in oestrus knows how to call musth mature, powerful bulls individually
- food:
	- outcompete most herbivores -- bark, fruit, roots, leaves, grasses
	- molars grind forward and backward (not side to side)
	- eat 4-6% of body weight daily (5000kg bull eats 300kg). Surprisingly little for such a large animal. 
	- feed 16hrs / day; can go days without drinking
	- drinks 200l per day (19l per trunk)
- social org:
	- matriarchal with herds of related female; males males separate in herds or alone
	- matriarch (largest cow) sets pace, activity and direction
	- males leave herd at adolescence (12-13 years). Separation is gradual -- adolescents may follow herd at distance. Female intolerance of boisterousness of sexual maturity
		- after independence, bulls alternate between bachelor herds and wandering alone
		- bulls wander more than cows
- behaviour:
	- when disturbed, group clusters around matriarch
	- altruistic: often help injured (although old/feeble females abandoned by the herd)
	- when two bulls meet, they measure each other up face-on with heads raised, tusks engaged. Usually
- communication: 
	- infrasound rumbling of stomach at low frequency, over several kms (up to 16km);
- reproduction:
	- conception at ~10 years; birth interval 4-9 years; gestation 22 months
	- males check females reproductive status by smell (trunk near genital)
	- during estrus, female more wary of bulls. If persistently approached by bull, she wil lrun away and circle back to herd later. If bull gets within touching distance, will mount.
	- bulls reach *social* maturatity at ~38yrs
- young:
	- cow gives birth close to family
	- strong maternal bond
	- weaned after 2-3 years; related calves suckle  
	- unlike most antelope, calves do not bump the mothers mammaries when they are drinking
	- at watering holes, female and calf in and out. Males drink, wallow

#### Buffalo
- social organization
	- stable groups of cows that are arranged in a dominance hierarchy, to which are attached adult and subadult bulls, ranked accordning to age and dominance
	- maternal bond with males ends after adolescence (3 years), after which they form bachelor groups. Cows maintain maternal bond until they become mothers (3-4 years)
- food
	- drink every 24hrs
- reproduction
	- 11.5 months gestation
	- bulls monitor female reproductive status by licking vulva, triggering urine tasting
	- cow coming into heat is guarded by dominant bull
- birth:
	- calves feeble for several hours, weak/slow for several weeks. Herd defensive strategy.

#### Giraffe
- jugular valves in neck: mechanical valves that close when upside down gravity (when giraffe drinks / grazes)
- while ruminating, can see cud balls slide up and down neck if you look closely
- when bending over to graze/drink, they throw their head down to use momentum against their **nuchal ligament**.
	- A thick, elastic neck tendon acts like a rubber band pulling the neck up.
	- This tendon strongly resists stretching when the giraffe tries to bend down.
	- Throwing the head down generates momentum to force the stiff tendon to stretch.
	- The stretched tendon then acts like a loaded spring to pull the head back up quickly if predators attack
#### Zebra
- social: harem herds, bachelor herds
	- to found a harem, stallion must first abduct a filly from her herd. Then he fends off rival stallions everytime she comes into heat (monthly). 
	- filly takes ~1 year before conceiving, so will likely go through multiple stallions before conceiving.
	- harem members acquired through conquest
	- fitter stallions will displace harem owners (normally peacefully)
- reproduction:
	- gives birth close to harem. Walks within hours. 
	- mother keeps all other members at distance for several days while foal learns mothers scent, stripes, voice
- movement:
	- rank hieararchy in order of time of joining harem -- walk in this file:
		- top mare (and her fowl) 1st, number 2 mare second etc.
		- stallion at rear

#### Spotted Hyaena
- females giving birth for first time suffer 9% mortality; 60% of young are stillborn
	- female genitals slightly masculinised (high testerone levels) -- birth canal bent at right angles and is much longer than umbilical cord so young are cut off from oxygen
	- nearly always give birth to twins
		- with incisor teeth fully erupted, immediate fight for dominance
		- if twins are both female, fight is invariably fatal for one
	- young suckle for 15-18 months (until they can follow on hunting trips)
		- reliance on milk: mother can hunt / forage for up to two days -- impractical to bring meat back
