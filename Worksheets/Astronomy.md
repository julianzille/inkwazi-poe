### Astronomy Worksheet

1. Universe ~13.8 billion years old
   Earth ~4.54 billion years old
2. Astronomy is the scientific study of stars, planets, galaxies and the universe
   Astrology is the belief that celestial bodies influence human behaviour (not a science)
3. Lightyear: the distance light travels in one year (9.46 trillion km)
4. A collection of stars, gas, dust and dark matter held together by gravity
5. Milky Way is the galaxy which our solar system is part of. Has a milky white appearance across the night sky
6. A truly full moon occurs when Earth is between the Sun and the Moon, with the Moon's Earth facing side fully illuminated. The exact datetime is calculated astronomically
7. A solar eclipse is caused when the **Moon** casts its shadow on Earth. If you are in the **umbra**, you experience a **total solar eclipse**. When you are in the **penumbra**, only part of the Sun is blocked and you experience a **partial solar eclipse**. If you are outside the **penumbra**, no eclipse is experienced.
8. Gibbous moon is when more than half but less than all of the moon is visible.
   Waning moon is when the illuminated portion gets smaller
   Waxing moon is when the illuminated portion gets bigger
9. A planet does not produce its own visible light, and orbits a star. A star produces its own light through nuclear fusion.
10. Sun - Mercury - Venus - Earth - Mars - *Asteroid belt (with Ceres)* - Jupiter - Saturn - Uranus - Neptune - *Kuiper Belt (with Pluto)*
11. 
    - Venus: 
	    - hottest planet in the Solar System. 
	    - Rotates backwards compared with most planets.
    - Mercury 
	    - Closest planet to the Sun. 
	    - Has the shortest year: 88 Earth days.
    - Mars
	    - Has the largest volcano in the Solar System, Olympus Mons.
	    - Has two small moons: Phobos and Deimos.
	- Jupiter
		- Largest planet in the Solar System.
		- Has the Great Red Spot, a gigantic storm.
	- Saturn
		- Famous for its spectacular ring system.
		- Has a very low density — it would float in water if a sufficiently large ocean existed.
12. 88 officially recognised constellations
13. Satellite appears as steady-moving point of light. Largest is ISS. Iridium flare is a brief, very bright flash caused by sunlight reflecting off the highly reflective antennae of Iridium satellites.
