4. Acacia is now "vachelia" or "senegalia":
	   - releases bitter tannins when browsed which deter browsers from eating leaves. The crushed leaves also release ethylene into air, warning nearby trees (of same species) of browsing activity, causing them to release tannins themselves
	   - thorns deter browsing activity

6. 967 grass species in Southern Africa (10% of world)
7. Grazing value of grasses: potential to produce palatable plant material
   Grasses react to grazing and other external disturbances by increasing (type 1, 2A, 2B, 2C) or decreasing in abundance
   Increaser (1-3): increase in abundance the more intense the disturbance ie. 1 increases in abundance in response to selective grazing, 3 increases in abundance when grass is overgrazed
8. Palatibility: how digestable plant matter is to grazers
9. Soil erosion causes fertile soils and nutrients get washed away, increased flooding and foliage wash-off, reducing ability to yield grass
10. Increaser species: increase in abundance when veld is grazed (or other external pressure such as drought)
    Decreaser species: decrease in abundance when veld is under similar pressure
    Invader: plants of exotic origin. May outcompete indigenous species
11. Veld burnt to 
    - burn off unpalatable moribund that accumulates and smothers plants, 
    - control bush encroachment of undesirable plants
    - reduce fuel for wildfires
