# Dangerous Animal Behavior

| Animal    | Aware / Encounter                                                                                                                      | Warning / Confrontation                                                                                                                | Fight / Engagement                                                                                      |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Crocodile | - tracks with eyes<br>- nostrils visible<br>- will most likely move away unless nesting                                                | - inflates to make self bigger<br>- hisses / belows                                                                                    | - lunges, clamps jaw, death rolls                                                                       |
| Hippo     | - stares @ threat<br>- submerges<br>- [maybe] vocal alarm call                                                                         | - yawns to show tusks<br>- loud grunting<br>- water scooping with mouth<br>- sinks head                                                | - charges fast with jaw open                                                                            |
| Lion      | - freezes with intense stare at threat<br>- ears raised, pointed in direction of threat                                                | - shows teeth, growls<br>- flattens ears<br>- flashes tip of tail                                                                      | - loud growling and bounding charging<br>- slaps paws when close                                        |
| Leopard   | - crouches flat on ground (camo)<br>- fixes attention on threat<br>- points ears at threat                                             | - shows teeth<br>- rasping growl<br>- ears partially back                                                                              | - charges with erratic running pattern<br>- growling<br>- while biting, will claw stomach with hindpaws |
| Rhino     | - will pause feeding<br>- ears pricked up with a lot of movement (scanning)<br>- tail may curl upward<br>- head in air to smell threat | - puffs/exhales loudly<br>- holds head low<br>- tail curls up<br>- may wipe horn on ground                                             | - charges, lowers head just before contact                                                              |
| Buffalo   | - stops feeding, turns towards threat<br>- raises head high<br>- intense stare at threat                                               | - herds may bunch together<br>- scratches ground, <br>- loud snorts                                                                    | - charges fast<br>- lowers head just before contact                                                     |
| Elephant  | - raises trunk to smell<br>- may not turn to threat, but will maintain awareness (sight, sound, smell)                                 | - ears out, raises head to make bigger<br>- trumpeting<br>- dust / vegetation throwing<br>- head twisting                              | - tail raised<br>- charges, ears may be out or pinned trumpeting, head lower                            |
| Snakes    | - freeze or slide away to avoid being seen<br>- increased tongue flicking (scent of threat)                                            | - hisses<br>- some prop up                                                                                                             | - strikes with fangs extended                                                                           |
| Hyaena    | - walk perimeter with head lower, ears pricked up, staring at threat<br>- may "call"                                                   | - ears flattened back<br>- may aggressively clap teeth and chomp air<br>- raises hairs on back<br>- vocalises with laughter sounds<br> | - fast charge with tail up<br>- lots of vocal noise                                                     |
