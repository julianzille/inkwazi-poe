# Social Structure
- more social than black rhino
- females and young associate in groups; adult males are solitary and territorial -- they seek out males to check reproductive status and stick around long enough to mate
- adult males tolerant of other males, so long as they're submissive
- females accompanied by their latest calf, which gets rejected at 2-3 years when mother seeks another companion
	- calf will then seek a companion of same sex+age,
	- or attach itself to another cow and calf
# Reproduction
