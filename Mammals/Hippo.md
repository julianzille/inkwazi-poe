
# Features	
- Core characteristics:
	- young can nurse underwater
	- nocturnal activity	
	- dung showering
- no scent or sweat glands, but mucous glands secrete fluid that protects thin epidermis from water loss and sunburn
# Ecology
- two essential requirements for survival: 
	- (i) water deep enough to submerge and 
	- (ii) nearby grassland
# Behavior
- mature hippos can stay underwater for 5 mins. Rise to breath (even in their sleep -- involuntary action). When underater, nostrils close and ears fold

# Social structure
- highly gregarious, contact species in water (during day), but solitary when foraging during night (exception -- mother and offspring)
- territorial (or alpha) bulls tolerate bachelor males so long as they behave submissively
# Reproduction
- 9 months gestation, conveive at 9 years with 2 year birthing interval. Bulls mature at >20years
- pregnant cows savagely protective
- peak copulations in dry-season (peak births during wet season)
- young adapted for nursing underwater -- ears fold and nostrils close while suckling on land
