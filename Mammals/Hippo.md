# Features
- Semi-aquatic megaherbivore
- Mucous gland secretions ("blood sweat"): reddish pigmented fluid acting as natural sunscreen, antibiotic, and moisture barrier for delicate skin
- Nostrils and ears possess muscular valves that seal automatically when submerged

# Behaviour
- Mature hippos submerge for up to 5 minutes; resurface to breathe automatically (even while asleep)
- Dung paddling / showering with paddle-shaped tail to mark territory and water entry trails
- Nocturnal terrestrial grazing; diurnal aquatic resting

# Social Structure
- Highly gregarious in water ("schools" or pods of 10-30), but solitary when grazing at night (except mother and calf)
- Dominant territorial bulls defend river/pan stretches; tolerate bachelor males as long as submissive posture is maintained

# Food & Diet
- Strict nocturnal grazers feeding on short lawn grasses (*Cynodon*, *Panicum*) up to several kilometers from water

# Reproduction & Offspring
- 9 month gestation; females conceive at ~9 years with 2-year birth interval; bulls mature >20 years
- Calves adapted to nurse underwater (ears fold and nostrils close during underwater suckling)
- Peak copulation occurs during dry season; peak calving during wet season
- Mothers vigorously protective of newborn calves against crocodiles and territorial bulls

# Ecology
- Key ecosystem engineers: create deep underwater hippo paths, maintain grazing lawns, and cycle vast quantities of organic nutrients into aquatic systems
