# Species

Lebombo wattle (*Newtonia hildebrandtii*)
- canopy tree -- abundant in Sand Forest
- wine-red pods, flattened, elongated
- paired

Lavender feverberry (*Croton gratissimis*)

Balloon thorn (*Vachellia luederitzii*)
- slender ant lays eggs in thorns
	- injects the thorn with growth hormone
	- symbiosis: reproductive housing for ants, trees protected from browsers (ants bite mammals)

Scented-pod thorn (*Acacia nilotica*)
- straight, white spines raked backward (outside tent)
- rough, fissured, grey-black bark
- pods beaded, red-brown

Num-num (*Carissa bispinossa*)
- forked spines (*bispinosa* -- "two spines")
- fruit eaten by people, monkeys, birds

Magic guarri (*Euclea divinorum*)
- *divinorum* -- "magic"
- wavy leaves

Ribbed currant (*Searsia pallens*)
- trifoliolate leaves
- orange lenticels

Thorny karee (Searsia guenzii)
- trifoliolate leaves (more shiny than ribbed currant)
- spiny stem

Tamboti (*Spirostachys africana*)
- Spirostachys: spiral + stachys (spike)
	- flowers arranged on tight spiral on a spike
	- 3-lobed fruit
- poisonous bark (+ smoke)
- nibbled on at base by porcupine
- dark brown, cracked barked

White stemmed guarri (*Euclea crispa*)
- whiteish silver stem

Sneezewood (*Ptaeroxylon obliquum*)
- leaves compound, opposite
- sawdust causes severe sneezing
- incredibly smooth

Pepper bark (*Warburgia salutaris*)
- edible leaves -- harvested for food / tea
- medicinal properties
- highly endangered (overharvested)

Elephant's Pudding (*Cissus rotundafolia*)
- evergreen succulent vine
