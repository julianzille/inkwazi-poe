# Species Notes

### Phinda Rain Frog (*Breviceps mossambicus* / *Breviceps* sp.)
- Discovered at Phinda by an &Beyond guide noticing distinct, atypical mating calls
- Direct development: terrestrial egg nests that bypass the aquatic tadpole stage

### Southern Foam Nest Frog (*Chiromantis xerampelina*)
- **Thermoregulation**: Changes colour as a thermoregulatory adaptation -- chalky white / pale during hot sunny days (reflects solar radiation); dark grey/brown at night or when cool (maximizes heat absorption)
- **Breeding**: Mates build communal whipped mucous foam nests on tree branches overhanging water pans
